R4 · {{閘門||Gates}}

{{在估計器之後，加兩道閘門||Two gates after the estimator}}

{{同一份資料，以 bioreactor 的節奏（每 30 秒一筆，六種起點全部平均）重播五種回報方式：||The same data replayed at the bioreactor cadence (one reading per 30 s, all six phase offsets pooled) through five reporting pipelines:}}

  1. P0 {{upper，每筆都報（10/10 介面上的做法）||upper, every reading reported (what the UI did on 10 Oct)}}
  2. P1 {{mad，每筆都報（v3.10.9 在 bioreactor 的預設）||mad, every reading reported (the v3.10.9 default in the bioreactor)}}
  3. P2 {{P1 ＋ 韌體旗標：無效就沿用上一筆有效值||P1 + firmware flag: an invalid reading repeats the last valid value}}
  4. P3 {{P2 ＋ 離散閘門：|mad − p90| > 0.03 OD 也視為無效||P2 + spread gate: |mad − p90| > 0.03 OD is also invalid}}
  5. P4 {{P3 ＋ 跳動閘門：和上一筆接受值差超過 max(0.05, 5%) 先不接受，連續 3 筆一致才接受新水準；輸出最近 3 筆接受值的中位數||P3 + jump gate: a reading more than max(0.05, 5%) from the last accepted value is held back, and a new level is accepted only after 3 agreeing readings; the output is the median of the last 3 accepted values}}

{{「穩態」＝每次加樣 150 秒之後，排除 11:08–11:22 的空氣事件。「延遲」＝加樣後第一次回到新水準 ±0.02 之內的時間（中位數）。氣泡測試含氣柱讀值，所以 P2、P3 擋不住。||"Steady" = more than 150 s after an addition, excluding the 11:08–11:22 air event. "Lag" = time after an addition until the output first comes within ±0.02 of the new level (median). The bubble test includes the slug readings, which is why P2 and P3 do not hold.}}

{{P0 與 P4，30 秒節奏||P0 against P4 at the 30 s cadence}}

{{左：V1 氣泡測試。右：V1 在主實驗裡 11:04 起的空氣事件（6 分鐘的氣柱）||left: V1 bubble test. right: V1's air event in the main run from 11:04 (a 6-minute slug)}}

{{右圖是 P4 的極限：氣柱持續超過 90 秒時，連續 3 筆一致的新水準會被接受。這時 P4 應該發出「無法解釋的跳動」警報，而不是默默地接受。生長中的菌液 30 秒內不會變 0.05 OD，所以任何被接受的大跳動都值得人去看。||The right panel shows P4's limit: when a slug lasts longer than 90 s, three agreeing readings make the new level acceptable. {{這些門檻是用同一份資料定的。τ = 0.03 是看乾淨讀值的最大離散（V1 0.026）選的；跳動門檻與 K = 3 是先驗選的，沒有調。下一次 bioreactor 跑是樣本外驗證：開跑前把 τ、跳動門檻、K 寫進預註記，不再改。||These thresholds were set on the same data. τ = 0.03 was chosen from the largest spread among clean readings (0.026 on V1); the jump threshold and K = 3 were chosen beforehand and not tuned.
