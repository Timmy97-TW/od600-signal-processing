A1 · {{四個估計器||The four estimators}}

{{同樣 14 個樣本，四種「取代表值」的方法||The same 14 samples, four ways to pick one value}}

{{每次量測，LED 亮起，感測器連續讀 16 次，丟掉前 2 次，剩 14 個數字。估計器的工作是把這 14 個數字變成一個。四個估計器的第一步都一樣：先找中位數。差別在接下來相信哪些樣本。||In each measurement the LED turns on, the sensor reads 16 times, the first 2 are dropped, and 14 numbers remain. The estimator's job is to turn those 14 into one. All four begin the same way, by finding the median. They differ in which samples they then trust.}}

{{估計器||Estimator}}{{怎麼決定「正常範圍」||How it sets the normal range}}{{怎麼用樣本||How it uses the samples}}{{一句話||In one line}}
mad E-1
{{現行||current}}		{{MAD：每個樣本到中位數的距離，再取中位數||MAD: the median of each sample's distance from the median}}{{中位數 ± 3×MAD 以內的全部平均，以外的丟掉||averages everything within median ± 3×MAD, discards the rest}}{{兩邊對稱地切掉離群值||cuts outliers symmetrically on both sides}}
qn E-2{{Qn：所有「兩兩差距」裡偏小的那一個分位數||Qn: a low quantile of all pairwise differences}}{{同 mad，只是門檻寬度用 Qn 算||as mad, with the gate width computed from Qn}}{{換一把尺量離散度，切法不變||a different ruler for spread, the same cut}}
biweight E-5{{MAD（同 mad）||MAD (as in mad)}}{{不是「留或丟」，而是離中心越遠權重越低，到 4.685×MAD 降為 0||no keep-or-drop: weight falls with distance from the centre and reaches 0 at 4.685×MAD}}{{軟性降權，不硬切||soft down-weighting instead of a hard cut}}
upper E-3{{MAD，只用來數有幾個被判成氣泡||MAD, used only to count how many are flagged as bubbles}}{{只平均「大於等於中位數」的那一半||averages only the half at or above the median}}{{只相信比較亮的樣本，因為氣泡只會讓光變暗||trusts only the brighter samples, because bubbles can only darken}}

{{重複 4000 個 burst：典型誤差，以及有多少次讀錯||4000 bursts repeated: the typical error, and how often a reading is wrong}}

{{每格是「誤差的中位數 · 誤差超過 0.05 OD 的讀值比例」，已乘光程係數 5。色塊越深代表越常讀錯||each cell is median error · share of readings off by more than 0.05 OD, path factor of 5 applied. darker shading means wrong more often}}

{{三個重點。第一，mad、qn、biweight 在四種情況下幾乎一樣。第二，氣泡一多，這三個讀錯的比例一起上升到約 18%，upper 不到一半；氣泡少的時候四個都很少讀錯。第三，upper 在乾淨時有一個小的固定偏移（通道估計值偏高約 0.75σ）：只要空白也用 upper 量，偏移會互相抵消；空白若用別的估計器量，偏移就留在 OD 裡。||Three points. First, mad, qn and biweight are nearly identical in all four cases. Second, with many bubbles those three go wrong together about 18% of the time and upper less than half as often; with few bubbles all four are rarely wrong. Third, upper carries a small fixed offset when clean (the channel estimate sits about 0.75σ high): it cancels as long as the blank was also taken with upper, and stays in the OD if the blank was taken with another estimator.}}

{{今天最容易犯的錯：空白跟估計器不配對。韌體只存一個 blankRatio，是「按下校正空白當時」的估計器算出來的。切到另一個估計器後，OD 仍然用舊的空白。所以今天的 OD 不看介面上的數字，而是事後用 CSV 裡的 lux_sample、lux_ref 重算：每個估計器用它自己在 n = 0 的比值當空白。||The easiest mistake today: a blank that does not match the estimator. The firmware stores one blankRatio, computed by whichever estimator was active when the blank button was pressed. After switching estimator, OD is still computed against that old blank. So today's OD is not read off the interface. It is recomputed afterwards from lux_sample and lux_ref in the CSV, with each estimator using its own ratio at n = 0 as its blank.}}

A2 · {{實驗怎麼做||How the assay runs}}

{{一杯水，一個循環，三台儀器||One beaker, one loop, three instruments}}

{{依 SOP v1.1（2026-10-10）。每次先取走 1 mL、再加 1 mL 工作液，總體積維持 50 mL。||Following SOP v1.1 (10 Oct 2026). Each cycle removes 1 mL and then adds 1 mL of working stock, holding the total at 50 mL.}}

{{兩台光度計串聯在同一條管路上，讀到的是同一份液體。BioDrop 讀的是從燒杯取出的 1 mL。三個讀值來自同一個濃度狀態 Cn，可以一點對一點比較。||The two photometers sit in series on one line and see the same liquid. The BioDrop reads the 1 mL taken from the beaker. All three readings come from the same state Cn and can be paired point by point.}}

{{開始前（約 30 分鐘）||Before you start (about 30 minutes)}}

{{試劑||Reagent}}

  1. {{原液（25 mg/mL）先 vortex 30 秒。||Vortex the stock (25 mg/mL) for 30 s.}}
  2. {{6 mL 原液 + 54 mL DI 水 → 60 mL 工作液（2.5 mg/mL）。||6 mL stock + 54 mL DI water → 60 mL of working stock (2.5 mg/mL).}}
  3. {{工作液每次吸取前都要 vortex。微球每小時沉約 3 mm，放著不動濃度就不對了。||Vortex the working stock before every draw. The beads sink about 3 mm per hour; left standing, the concentration is wrong.}}

{{管路與空白||Loop and blank}}

  1. {{先量管路體積 vt。空管路接好後，量筒裝 DI 水灌滿管路與兩個 flow cell，記下用掉幾 mL。||Measure the hold-up volume vt first. With the empty loop connected, fill the tubing and both flow cells from a measuring cylinder and note the mL used.}}
  2. {{燒杯加 50 mL DI 水，開攪拌、開幫浦（約 60 mL/min），趕走所有氣泡。||Add 50 mL DI water to the beaker, start stirring and the pump (about 60 mL/min), and clear every bubble.}}
  3. {{循環 3 分鐘後，記下兩台的 sample 與 reference（lux）。reference 應 ≥ 250 lx，不到就先調光路。||After 3 min of circulation note sample and reference (lux) on both. Reference should be ≥ 250 lx; if not, adjust the optics first.}}
  4. {{兩台都按「校正空白」，確認介面頂端的黃色提示消失。||Press "calibrate blank" on both and confirm the yellow banner at the top has gone.}}
  5. {{四個估計器各量 3 筆空白（切換方法見下）。這就是各自的空白比值。||Take 3 blank readings with each of the four estimators (switching described below). These are their individual blank ratios.}}
  6. {{從燒杯取 1 mL DI 水當 BioDrop 的空白。不要補水，燒杯現在是 49 mL。||Take 1 mL of DI water from the beaker as the BioDrop blank. Do not top up; the beaker now holds 49 mL.}}

{{介面設定（兩台都要）||Interface settings (on both)}}

  • {{按「開始錄製」。今天所有數字都從板上的 CSV 來，不靠手抄。||Press "start recording". All of today's numbers come from the CSV on the board, not from hand copying.}}
  • {{取樣時長（＝OD 量測間隔）設 5 秒，光程係數維持 5。||Set the sampling window (which is the OD interval) to 5 s; keep the path factor at 5.}}
  • {{準備一張紙本時間表：每次加樣的時刻與 n。CSV 沒有 n 這一欄，事後靠時間對回去。||Keep a paper time sheet: the clock time and n of every addition. The CSV has no column for n; it is matched back by time.}}
  • {{若光纖簡化版沒有「演算法切換」，就讓它用自己的演算法全程錄製，並在時間表上註明。||If the simplified fibre version has no algorithm switch, let it record throughout with its own algorithm and note that on the time sheet.}}
