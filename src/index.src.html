01 · {{從零開始||From zero}}

{{OD600 量的不是「吸收」，是「光沒有抵達偵測器」||OD600 does not measure absorption. It measures light that failed to arrive.}}

{{把一束 600 nm 的光射過菌液。細菌幾乎不吸收這個波長的光，它們做的事是把光散射到別的方向。偵測器只看得到沒被打偏的那部分，所以菌越多、訊號越小。||Shine a 600 nm beam through a culture. Bacteria absorb almost none of it. What they do is scatter it in other directions. The detector only sees the part that was not deflected, so more cells means less signal.}}

T = I / I₀      OD = −log₁₀(T) = log₁₀(I₀ / I)

{{I₀ 是空白（只有培養基）時的光強，I 是有菌時的光強。取 log 是因為每多一層菌，光是「乘上」一個比例而不是「減掉」一個量，log 把乘法變成加法，OD 才會跟濃度成正比。||I₀ is the intensity through a blank (medium only); I is the intensity through the culture. The log is there because each added layer of cells multiplies the light by a fraction instead of subtracting an amount. The log turns that product into a sum, which is why OD is proportional to concentration.}}

{{動手試：把濃度往上推||Try it: push the concentration up}}

{{看「偵測器收到的 counts」怎麼掉，以及 OD 的解析度怎麼跟著變差||Watch the detector counts fall and the OD resolution fall with them}}

{{最後一格是重點：偵測器只能分辨整數 count。訊號剩越少，一個 count 相當的 OD 就越大。這就是為什麼高 OD 端永遠比較吵，也是為什麼「空白時有多少 counts」決定了整台儀器的上限。||The last box is the point. A detector resolves whole counts. The less signal is left, the more OD one count is worth. That is why the high end is always noisier, and why the number of counts at blank sets the ceiling for the whole instrument.}}

{{為什麼不同儀器讀值不同||Why instruments disagree}}

{{被散射的光有一部分仍然朝前，偵測器張角越大就收進越多，讀到的 OD 越低。所以 OD 是「這台儀器的幾何」的函數，不是樣品的絕對性質。||Some scattered light still travels forward. A detector with a wider acceptance angle collects more of it and reads a lower OD. OD is therefore a property of the instrument's geometry as well as of the sample.}}

{{為什麼高濃度會失準||Why high density bends the curve}}

{{濃度高時，光被散射好幾次，有些又被打回偵測器。讀值開始低於直線外推，這叫多重散射。它是物理現象，演算法修不掉，只能用校正曲線描述並宣告有效範圍。||At high density light is scattered several times, and some of it is steered back into the detector. Readings fall below the straight line. This is multiple scattering: physics, which no algorithm removes. It can only be described by a calibration curve with a stated valid range.}}

{{為什麼需要標準品||Why a standard is needed}}

{{細菌會長、會變形、會死。要分辨「儀器的偏差」與「生物的變化」，需要一種不會變的散射體。大小接近細菌的 SiO₂ 微球就是這個角色。||Cells grow, change shape and die. Separating instrument bias from biology needs a scatterer that does not change. Silica microspheres close to bacterial size play that role.}}

03 · {{偏差從哪裡來||Where bias comes from}}

{{六個機制，每一個都可以先算出方向與大小||Six mechanisms, each with a direction and size you can predict}}

{{先有預測、再做實驗，量到的東西才叫驗證。下面每張圖都可以拖動。||Predict first, then measure: only then is the result a test. Every figure below is interactive.}}

3.1 {{count 不夠：解析度被感測器吃掉||Count starvation: resolution lost at the sensor}}

{{通道上只有 N 個 counts 時，它變動 1 count 造成的 OD 變化是：||With N counts on a channel, a one-count change moves the OD by:}}

ΔOD = k · log₁₀((N + 1) / N) ≈ 0.434 · k / N      (k = {{光程係數||path factor}} = 5)

{{一個 count 值多少 OD||What one count is worth in OD}}

{{兩個標記是我們自己兩次運轉的 reference 通道：434 h 那次約 32 counts，102 h 那次約 265 counts。光是把 reference 調亮，一個 count 的代價就從 0.067 降到 0.008 OD。||The two markers are the reference channel in our own two runs: about 32 counts in the 434 h run and about 265 in the 102 h run. Brightening the reference alone cut the cost of one count from 0.067 to 0.008 OD.}}

3.2 {{平均與 log 的順序||The order of averaging and the log}}

{{log 是往下彎的曲線，所以「先各自取 log 再平均」一定比「先平均再取 log」讀得高。差距只跟光強的相對雜訊 σr 有關：||The log curve bends downward, so taking the log of each sample and then averaging always reads higher than averaging first. The gap depends only on the relative noise of the intensity, σr:}}

mean(ODi) − OD(mean I) ≈ k · σr² / (2 ln 10) = 0.217 · k · σr²

{{雜訊越大，兩種算法差越多||The noisier the signal, the wider the gap}}

{{乾淨的 cuvette（σr ≈ 2%）幾乎沒差。有氣泡的循環管路（30% 以上）就差到百分之一 OD 以上。規則很簡單：所有統計都在光強上做完，最後只取一次 log。||In a clean cuvette (σr ≈ 2%) the gap is negligible. In a bubbly loop (30% and above) it exceeds a hundredth of an OD. The rule is simple: finish all the statistics on intensity, then take the log once.}}

3.3 {{氣泡只會擋光，不會加光||Bubbles only remove light}}

{{這種干擾是單邊的。若一個 burst 裡有比例 f 的樣本只剩 k 倍的光，單純平均會多報：||The disturbance is one-sided. If a fraction f of the samples in a burst keep only a fraction k of the light, a plain mean over-reads by:}}

ΔOD = −kpath · log₁₀(1 − f · (1 − k))

{{四種估計器面對同一串氣泡||Four estimators facing the same bubbles}}

{{每次 2000 個模擬 burst，每個 burst 14 個樣本，真實 OD 固定||2000 simulated bursts of 14 samples each, true OD held fixed}}

{{長條是平均偏差，細線是單次讀值的 ±1 SD，都已乘上光程係數 5。「上側分位帶」取每個 burst 裡最亮的 60–90% 區段，物理意義是光路最沒被擋到的時刻。f 接近 50% 時中位數也會垮。||Bars are mean bias, whiskers are ±1 SD of a single reading, both already multiplied by the path factor of 5. The upper band takes the 60th–90th percentile of each burst: the moments when the path was least obstructed. As f nears 50% the median collapses too.}}

3.4 {{離群值濾波遇到量化資料||An outlier gate meets quantised data}}

{{MAD 是「離中位數的距離的中位數」。當 14 個樣本裡有一半以上是同一個整數，MAD 就是 0，3×MAD 的門檻也是 0，任何不等於中位數的樣本都被當成氣泡丟掉。濾波器退化成「取眾數」，本來可以靠平均得到的小數點解析度就沒了。||The MAD is the median distance from the median. When more than half of 14 samples are the same integer, the MAD is 0, so the 3×MAD gate is 0, and every sample that differs from the median is discarded as a bubble. The filter degenerates into taking the mode, and the sub-count resolution that averaging would have given is lost.}}

{{真實光強從 32 慢慢升到 33 counts，估計器回報什麼||True level rising slowly from 32 to 33 counts: what each estimator reports}}

{{理想||ideal}}{{單純平均||plain mean}}{{目前韌體（MAD 門檻下限 10⁻⁶）||current firmware (MAD floor 10⁻⁶)}}

{{橘線是階梯：輸入連續變化，輸出卻一格一格跳。這個濾波器回報的「bubble %」在這種情況下數的其實是量化閃爍，不是氣泡。修法是替 MAD 設一個合理的下限（一個 count），圖上會跟藍線重合。把雜訊拉大到 1 count 以上，兩條線自己就會靠攏。||The orange line is a staircase: the input moves smoothly, the output jumps. In this regime the "bubble %" the filter reports is counting quantisation flicker, not bubbles. The fix is a sensible floor for the MAD (one count), which lands on the blue line. Raise the noise above one count and the two lines converge by themselves.}}

3.5 {{漏光決定上限||Stray light sets the ceiling}}

{{只要有一小部分光不經過樣品就到了偵測器（繞過 cuvette、外殼漏光、沒扣乾淨的暗場），不管菌多濃，訊號都不會低於那個量：||If a small fraction of light reaches the detector without crossing the sample (around the cuvette, through the housing, or as an uncorrected dark level), the signal never drops below it however dense the culture:}}

ODapparent = −k · log₁₀(10−OD/k + s) + k · log₁₀(1 + s)      ODmax ≈ −k · log₁₀(s)

{{看起來的 OD 對真實 OD||Apparent OD against true OD}}

{{理想||ideal}}s = 0.1%s = 1%s = 5%

{{這種彎曲跟多重散射長得很像，都是高端讀低，但成因不同：漏光是儀器的，多重散射是樣品的。分辨方法是把同一個樣品稀釋後再量，或在光路上放不透光片直接量 s。||This bend looks like multiple scattering, since both read low at the top, but the causes differ: stray light belongs to the instrument, multiple scattering to the sample. Tell them apart by diluting the same sample, or by blocking the beam with an opaque slide and measuring s directly.}}

3.6 {{時間上的平滑會遲到||Smoothing in time arrives late}}

{{把連續讀值做指數移動平均（時間常數 τ）會讓曲線好看，代價是延遲。對以速率 μ 指數成長的訊號，平滑後的值永遠低 μτ/(1+μτ)，而且生長速率改變的時刻會晚大約 τ 才看得到。||An exponential moving average with time constant τ makes a curve look better and costs delay. For a signal growing exponentially at rate μ the smoothed value sits low by μτ/(1+μτ), and a change in growth rate shows up about τ late.}}

{{生長在第 10 小時停止：平滑器什麼時候才發現||Growth stops at hour 10: when does the smoother notice}}

{{真實||true}}{{平滑後||smoothed}}

{{我們的預警層靠的是及早看到生長速率轉折。所以規則是：存檔的 OD 永遠不平滑；平滑只能出現在估計層，而且要寫出它的延遲。||Our early-warning layer depends on seeing a change in growth rate early. So the rule: archived OD is never smoothed; smoothing lives only in the estimation layer, with its delay stated.}}

05 · {{標準品||The standard}}

{{1 µm SiO₂ 微球能證明什麼，不能證明什麼||What 1 µm silica microspheres can and cannot prove}}

{{能||It can}}

  • {{給出一條濃度已知、不會生長的階梯||provide a ladder of known concentration that does not grow}}
  • {{量出線性範圍、雜訊底、偵測極限||measure linear range, noise floor and limit of detection}}
  • {{在同一批原始資料上比較演算法||compare algorithms on one set of raw data}}
  • {{把讀值換算成等效顆粒數，讓不同實驗室可以比較||convert readings to equivalent particle counts, comparable across laboratories}}

{{不能||It cannot}}

  • {{告訴你每單位 OD 有多少細胞乾重||tell you the dry cell mass per unit OD}}
  • {{代表桿狀、成鏈、會變大小的 B. subtilis||stand in for a rod that chains and changes size}}
  • {{靜置超過幾分鐘（會沉）||sit still for more than a few minutes (it settles)}}
  • {{校正被菌膜蓋住的光窗||calibrate a window covered by biofilm}}

{{黃色標記是 Day 4 之後要填的數字。其餘每個數字都已經在第 04 節有出處。||Highlighted items are numbers to fill in after Day 4. Every other number is already sourced in section 04.}}

We audited the arithmetic inside our photometer before we trusted its curves. An OD600 value is the output of a chain of decisions: how many samples to take, which to discard, when to subtract the dark level, when to take the logarithm. Each decision carries a bias with a predictable direction, so we derived those biases first and then measured them.

The audit began with our own data. In our 434-hour culture the reference channel of the photometer sat at about 32 sensor counts. At that level a single count is worth 0.067 OD after our path-length factor of 5, and 91.5% of the 2,671 reference readings fell on an exact integer count. Between 40 and 230 hours the reference changed count 29 times, and 26 of those changes produced an OD jump larger than 0.04. Those jumps were made by the instrument. We traced part of this to our outlier filter: with quantised data its robust spread estimate collapses to zero, so the filter returns the most common count and discards the averaging that would have resolved fractions of a count.

To measure each bias we used 1 µm monodisperse silica microspheres as a scatterer that does not grow. We recorded every raw sensor sample once, over 12 concentrations from blank to [top] mg/mL, ascending by addition and descending by dilution, and under deliberate disturbances: injected bubbles, changed room light, a dimmed reference channel and changed flow. We then replayed that single data set through eight reduction algorithms. Because every algorithm saw identical samples, any difference between them is a difference in arithmetic alone. The algorithm list, the metrics and the selection rule were committed to our repository before the first measurement.

The replay gave [summary of D3: e.g. a plain mean over-read by X OD in the bubble arm, the upper-percentile estimator stayed within Y]. 我們先稽核光度計裡的運算，才相信它畫出來的曲線。一個 OD600 數值是一連串決定的產物：取幾個樣本、丟掉哪些、何時扣暗場、何時取 log。每個決定都帶有方向可預測的偏差，所以我們先推導，再實測。

稽核從自己的資料開始。434 小時那次培養，reference 通道約 32 counts；乘上光程係數 5 之後一個 count 值 0.067 OD，2,671 筆 reference 讀值有 91.5% 落在整數 count 上。40–230 小時之間 reference 換了 29 次 count，其中 26 次造成超過 0.04 的 OD 跳動。這些跳動是儀器造成的。部分原因在離群值濾波：資料量化後它的穩健離散估計變成零，於是回傳最常見的 count，丟掉了本來能解析小數 count 的平均。
