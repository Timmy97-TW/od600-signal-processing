{{校正與 OD 的物理||Calibration and the physics of OD}}

  1. Beal J. et al. (2020). Robust estimation of bacterial cell count from optical density. Communications Biology 3:512. doi:10.1038/s42003-020-01127-5
    {{iGEM 2018 InterLab，244 組資料。0.961 µm 單分散 SiO₂ 微球、四重複二倍連續稀釋。以微球校正的單細胞螢光與流式細胞儀差 1.07 倍，以 CFU 校正差 28.4 倍。LUDOX 的變異其實更小，但只有單點、沒有殘差可檢查，也無法換算顆粒數。文中引用的細菌折射率為 1.33–1.41。||iGEM InterLab 2018, 244 data sets. Monodisperse 0.961 µm silica microspheres in quadruplicate two-fold serial dilution. Per-cell fluorescence calibrated by microspheres was 1.07-fold from flow cytometry, against 28.4-fold for CFU. LUDOX was in fact slightly more precise, but it is a single point with no residuals to check and cannot give a particle count. Bacterial refractive index is quoted there as 1.33–1.41.}}
  2. Stevenson K., McVey A.F., Clark I.B.N., Swain P.S., Pilizota T. (2016). General calibration of microbial growth in microplate readers. Scientific Reports 6:38828. doi:10.1038/srep38828
    {{OD 只在單次散射區（約 OD ≲ 0.2）與細胞數成正比；不同儀器即使在該區也要互相校正；細胞變長時同樣細胞數的 OD 會改變。使用的是聚苯乙烯微球，不是 SiO₂。||OD is proportional to cell number only in the single-scattering regime (about OD ≲ 0.2); instruments need cross-calibration even there; elongated cells change OD at constant cell number. The beads used were polystyrene, not silica.}}
  3. Myers J.A., Curtis B.S., Curtis W.R. (2013). Improving accuracy of cell and chromophore concentration measurements using optical density. BMC Biophysics 6:4. doi:10.1186/2046-1682-6-4
    {{同一份 E. coli 懸浮液在兩台儀器上差超過 40%，染料只差 25%；差異歸因於偵測器的接收角。也示範了約 0.1 cm 光程的 LED flow cell。||The same E. coli suspension differed by more than 40% between two instruments while a dye differed by 25%; the difference is attributed to detector acceptance angle. Also demonstrates an LED flow cell with a path near 0.1 cm.}}
  4. Mira P., Yeh P., Hall B.G. (2022). Estimating microbial population data from optical density. PLoS ONE 17:e0276040. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0276040
    {{OD 與細胞數只到約 OD 0.1 成正比；兩種菌在幾乎相同的 OD（0.62 與 0.61）下細胞數差約 15 倍。||OD is proportional to cell titre only to about OD 0.1; two species at nearly the same OD (0.62 and 0.61) differed about 15-fold in cell number.}}
  5. Koch A.L. (1970). Turbidity measurements of bacterial cultures in some available commercial instruments. Analytical Biochemistry 38:252–259. doi:10.1016/0003-2697(70)90174-0
    {{儀器幾何影響濁度讀值的經典出處。未讀全文；相關數字請引用 3 與 6。||The classic source for instrument geometry affecting turbidity readings. Full text not read; take numbers from 3 and 6.}}
  6. Deutzmann J.S. et al. (2022). Low-cost clamp-on photometers (ClampOD) and tube photometers (TubeOD) for online cell density determination. Frontiers in Microbiology 12:790576. doi:10.3389/fmicb.2021.790576
    {{琥珀色 LED 加光電晶體的低成本線上光度計。把空白調到滿刻度的約 90% 才有完整線性範圍；偵測器輸出低於滿刻度約 0.3%（OD × 光程 > 2.5）後失準；裝置對溫度敏感。||Low-cost online photometers with an amber LED and a phototransistor. The blank must sit near 90% of full scale for the full linear range; accuracy fails once sensor output drops below about 0.3% of full scale (OD × path > 2.5); the devices are temperature sensitive.}}
